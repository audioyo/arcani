Arcani Architecture

One C++ engine builds every tone live from a recipe. The same samples come out on your PC, an Android phone, and an iPhone. Science lives in a fixed core. Character lives in a free-form outer layer.

The signal chain

Core: entrainment signals, fixed wiring Tone: track character, free routing Binaural: joins last, untouched

Two zones, one engine

Core zone

The theta reference, gamma PAC layer, and binaural pair. Designers move parameters inside locked, sourced ranges. Nobody rewires it.

Tone zone

Stems, music, ambience, and effects, routed freely per track. It runs on its own musical clock and never pulses at entrainment rates.

Binaural path

Two steady sines, one per ear. They skip every 3D, reverb, and width stage, and join the mix after all of them.

Rules that hold everything together

  • AD-1
    The engine is pure math. No devices, files, threads, or UI inside it. The CLI, designer, and apps are thin hosts around one small C interface.
  • AD-2
    Every recipe is checked before it plays. The validator turns it into a locked plan. Nothing else can reach the renderer.
  • AD-4
    Binaural joins last. The master chain never mixes left and right. A structure test and a signal test both guard this.
  • AD-5
    Gamma follows the theta reference only. The recipe has no field for any other target.
  • AD-7
    Plugins are baked, never live. Use any plugin in Reaper and print the stem. The phone plays the file.
  • AD-8
    3D is rendered by Arcani, not the phone. Gamma and stems move as objects. Theta and binaural stay still. Output is plain stereo, so Atmos or phone spatializers never touch the binaural pair.
  • AD-9
    Identical samples on every platform. Own math functions, integer phase, strict compiler flags, and hash checks across x64 and ARM in CI.
  • AD-17
    Every render carries a report. One assertion library serves the CLI, CI, and the designer's publish button. That report is how Claude checks the sound without ears.
  • AD-18
    A calm visualizer. Slow motion that follows the session arc. It never flashes at theta, gamma, or beat rates, and never more than 3 times a second.
  • AD-25
    Fresh stems every listen, reproducible every time. Tests and tools use the recipe seed. The app picks a new seed per play and keeps it on the phone, so any play can be rebuilt exactly.
  • AD-19
    One signal spec. Engine and Python reference both build from the same written formulas, so they can match to below -100 dBFS.
