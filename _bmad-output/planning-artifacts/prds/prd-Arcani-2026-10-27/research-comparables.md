# Comparable Apps: Quick Scan

Scanned 2026-10-27 from public store listings and vendor sites. Prices are US unless noted and change often. "Unverified" means I found no first-party source.

## Endel

- **Positioning:** "Personalized soundscapes to help you focus, relax, and sleep. Backed by neuroscience." ([endel.io](https://endel.io/)). Current US App Store subtitle: "ADHD Productivity Timer" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/endel-focus-sleep-relax/id1346247457)).
- **Features:** Generative soundscapes. Free: Focus, Sleep, Relax, Move. Premium: Colored Noise, Study, Dynamic Focus, Hibernation, Nature Elements, Recovery, Spatial Orbit, Focus Timer, plus artist packs (James Blake, Grimes, Plastikman, Miguel) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/endel-focus-sleep-relax/id1346247457)).
- **Split and price:** Free edition plus Premium. US in-app list runs $2.99 to $119.99, with $124.99 lifetime ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/endel-focus-sleep-relax/id1346247457)). A tracker reported cheaper tiers in Dec 2025 ([Visualping](https://visualping.io/pages/endel-pricing-alerts-4896407)), unverified.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Mac, Windows, Watch, Apple TV, Fire TV, Alexa, web ([endel.io](https://endel.io/)).
- **Claims:** "Up to 7x increase in focus," "3.6x decrease in stress," sourced to a bioRxiv whitepaper; FAQ hedges it "may not work for everyone" ([endel.io](https://endel.io/)). No medical disclaimer on the store page ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/endel-focus-sleep-relax/id1346247457)).
- **Notable:** Adapts to time, weather, heart rate, location, cadence. Binaural beats in one scenario only, stereo headphones required ([endel.io](https://endel.io/)). "All soundscapes are available offline." Spatial Orbit is "an immersive Spatial Audio experience" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/endel-focus-sleep-relax/id1346247457)). Sleep timer: not found.

## Brain.fm

- **Positioning:** "Music made for Deep Work" ([brain.fm](https://www.brain.fm/)). US subtitle: "ADHD focus, Insomnia, Anxiety" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brain-fm-focus-sleep-music/id1110684238)).
- **Features:** Focus, relax, meditate, sleep modes; genre choice; Pomodoro and interval timers; offline downloads ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brain-fm-focus-sleep-music/id1110684238)).
- **Split and price:** No permanent free tier; trial then subscription. Vendor blog cites $14.99/month or $99.99/year ([brain.fm blog](https://www.brain.fm/blog/is-brain-fm-worth-it)); App Store lists ten tiers from $6.99 to $99.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brain-fm-focus-sleep-music/id1110684238)).
- **Platforms:** Web, iOS, Android, desktop ([brain.fm](https://www.brain.fm/)).
- **Claims:** Strongest outcome language in the set: "Increase focus by up to 10x," "Reduce tension/anxiety by 2x" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brain-fm-focus-sleep-music/id1110684238)); "boost focus-associated beta brainwaves by 119%"; cites NSF funding and a Nature Communications paper; no medical disclaimer found ([brain.fm](https://www.brain.fm/)).
- **Notable:** Adjustable "neural effect" modulation levels, with a high setting marketed for ADHD ([brain.fm](https://www.brain.fm/)).

## Expand (Monroe Institute)

- **Positioning:** "Experience, Transform, Awaken" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expand-beyond-meditation/id1552705313)).
- **Features:** 100+ guided meditations, mini-courses, journaling, "custom-generated soundscapes" for brainwave states, a timer up to 2 hours ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expand-beyond-meditation/id1552705313)).
- **Split and price:** Free with some content; Expand Plus $11.99/month or $69.99/year ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expand-beyond-meditation/id1552705313)).
- **Platforms:** iOS, iPadOS, Mac, Vision Pro ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expand-beyond-meditation/id1552705313)). Android listed by a third party ([Similarweb](https://similarweb.com/es/app/google/org.monroeinstitute.expand)), unverified.
- **Claims:** "reduce stress, improve sleep, and cultivate greater meaning"; no medical disclaimer ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expand-beyond-meditation/id1552705313)).
- **Notable:** Offline downloads; a pre-course headphone and sound check ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expand-beyond-meditation/id1552705313)). Guided voice content, not a synth-first app.

- **Features:** 40 multi-stage binaural programs; 20 single beat rates from 1 to 100 Hz; noise and nature beds; mixes with other audio apps ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id307219387)).

## Takeaways

- **Table stakes:** focus, relax, sleep modes; offline play; session timers; a free taste before a subscription near $70 to $120 a year (all sources above).
- **Premium patterns match the plan:** Endel already gates artist packs and a spatial mode behind Premium ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/endel-focus-sleep-relax/id1346247457)). Arcani needs a sharper reason to pay than "more of the same."
- **Gap, transparency:** No app documents its signal by carrier, modulation type and rate. Brain.fm and Endel cite proprietary or "patented" methods (sources above). A published recipe format is open ground.
- **Gap, honest claims:** Competitors use multiplier claims ("10x," "7x") and ADHD or insomnia subtitles without disclaimers. Sound-only description is a differentiator and lowers store-review risk.
- **Gap, determinism:** No app claims that the same session renders identically every time. Unverified that any does.
- **Gap, accessibility:** Only BrainWave and Endel state headphone needs; none found offering a single-sided hearing mode or a left/right channel check. Expand's headphone check is the nearest feature.
- **Naming collisions to avoid:** "Spatial Orbit," "Dynamic Focus," "Hibernation" (Endel); "Neural Effect," "neural phase locking" (Brain.fm); "Expand," "Sound Science" and all Monroe Institute terms (Expand); "BrainWave" (Banzai Labs).
