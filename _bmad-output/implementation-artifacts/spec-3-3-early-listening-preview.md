## Intent

**Problem:** Every layer now exists and the four starter layer configurations pass the spacing rule (Story 3.2), but nobody has heard them. Every layer runs at unit amplitude until Epic 4, so a plain render of two layers peaks near 2.0 and clips. A harsh carrier or a rough gamma pulse would only be found at golden sign-off in Epic 6.

**Approach:** `arcani-render` gains a `--preview` flag that keeps a short opening of the recipe's own render, scaled by one gain so the summed layers peak at -30 dBFS, and marks the report as an unsigned preview. A tool renders six previews at 48 kHz into `build/previews/`, and a listening checklist records what Audio hears on earbuds and on headphones.

Decisions (approved 2026-11-17 by the orchestrator under Audio's delegation; Audio delegated every decision and approval for Stories 3.1 to 3.3 on 2026-11-16):

1. **Flag.** `arcani-render RECIPE --out OUT.wav --preview` makes a preview render. Measure mode only: `--preview` with `--mode final` is a usage error (exit 2, nothing written). Any `--rate` is allowed; the previews for listening are made at 48 kHz.
2. **Length.** New row `preview-length` 180 s. A non-looping recipe previews its first min(180 s, session) seconds. A looping recipe previews its opening, one whole body, and a tail of new row `preview-seam-tail` 30 s past the first body seam (Focus and Learn: 60 + 120 + 30 = 210 s), capped at the session length, so the opening-to-body seam and the first body-to-body seam are both heard. Frames are whole samples by the existing seconds-to-samples rule. The engine renders the recipe as usual; the CLI stops after the preview frames. The recipe hash and the plan are the recipe's own.
3. **Level.** Every layer runs at unit amplitude (signal spec, Level paragraphs), so the analytic sample peak of the summed layers in an ear is n, the number of layers present in that ear (theta reference, gamma PAC layer, and the binaural tone of that ear, with a muted binaural ear counting 0). The preview gain is g = 10^(P/20) / n_max, P = new row `preview-peak-level` -30 dBFS, n_max the larger ear count. It is an engineering default for previews only, logged in a new design log entry. g is applied in the CLI to each output sample (one binary64 multiply) before the 24-bit rounding; the engine and every non-preview byte are unchanged. To keep g identical on every platform with no transcendental call, P must be a whole multiple of 10 dB (checked when the row loads; other values are a table error), and 10^(P/20) is built from exact powers of ten and at most one correctly rounded `sqrt(10)` (for -30 dB: 1 / (10 * sqrt(10))), then one division by n_max. This is written in a new short `## Preview` section of `docs/signal-spec.md` with vector tables generated by `tools/signal_spec_vectors.py`.
4. **Report.** A preview report adds a `preview` member after `spacing` (or after `loop` when there is no spacing block): `{"signed": false, "peak_dbfs": -30, "layers_per_ear": n_max, "gain": g (shortest round-trip), "seconds": preview length}` and `frames` is the preview frame count. A non-preview report is byte-identical to today. The stderr and exit codes are unchanged otherwise.
5. **Never golden.** The previews are rendered by a new `tools/render_previews.py` into `build/previews/` (already gitignored) as `<fixture>.preview.wav` plus its report. It refuses (exit 2) any output directory inside the repository that is not under `build/`. Nothing in `ci/` or the hash probe reads previews.
6. **Preview set.** The four starter layer fixtures, plus two new checklist fixtures in `recipes/fixtures/valid/`, 180 s, no binaural layer: `preview-gamma-250-am-60-swell.json` and `preview-gamma-250-am-60-bursts.json`: a 450 Hz theta reference carrier amplitude-modulated by the sine envelope at 6 Hz, 75 percent depth, and a gamma PAC layer, a 250 Hz sine carrier amplitude-modulated at 60 Hz, 100 percent depth, coupled as smooth swell / 50 ms bursts at the 0 degree coupling phase. Both must pass the spacing rule and the carrier rule, with their margins recorded; if either fails, stop and report rather than change values.
7. **Listening checklist.** A new `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/listening-3-3-preview.md`: how to render (one command), the level note (previews peak at -30 dBFS; raise playback in Reaper to comfort), the budget (total audio per pass, two passes: earbuds and headphones; under about 1 hour of Audio's time), and a table per render with plain-word lines to listen for and empty Earbuds and Headphones observation columns. Required lines: each carrier's tone (harsh or not); the gamma pulses (rough or not); for Focus, the free-running gamma envelope inside the 50 ms bursts, where each burst opens on a different gamma phase (design log (qq), deferred-work row); the seams at the opening end and the first body seam for Focus and Learn (times in seconds); the Sleep binaural beat cycle; the 250 Hz carrier with gamma AM at 60 Hz, swell and bursts (rule 4 review condition 2). A closing section: "Proposed changes" (each routed to its owning story before Epic 4) and "Result" (including "nothing needs changing" as a valid result). Plain words, describe the sound, no outcome claims.
8. **Tests budget.** Story tests add about 1 minute or less to CI (full windows-x64 is near its 45 min limit). Contracts favor short fixtures and one full-length preview render at most.

### Choices made in this engagement (inside the frozen decisions)

1. **Peak level bounds.** Besides the whole multiple of 10 dB, row `preview-peak-level` must lie in [-440, 0] dB: above 0 dB the summed layers clip at 24-bit rounding, and 10^22 (P = -440) is the largest power of ten binary64 holds exactly, which also bounds the multiplication loop. Both bounds come from properties of the output format and of binary64, not from tuning.
2. **No layer.** `n_max` 0 (a silent render) counts as 1, so `g` is defined for every recipe; the output is silent anyway.
3. **`seconds`** in the report is `F / fs` (one binary64 division), the preview's own length (210 for Focus and Learn), not the row value.
4. **`loop` and `spacing` blocks** in a preview report are the plan's own, unchanged; `seam_frames` still lists every seam to the cap.
5. **Placement of `preview`:** after `spacing`, else after `loop`, else after `frames`; always before `determinism_key`, which stays the recipe's own.
6. **Table error exit.** A bad `preview-peak-level` is a table error found when the CLI reads the row, before compile and before any output: exit 3 (the existing file or internal error code), stderr `error: <TableError message>`.
7. **Layer count source.** The signal spec defines `n` from the recipe fields; the CLI may count distinct layers of each ear's component rows (`arc_plan_component`), which is the same number and honors a plan's muted ear.

**CLI helpers (new `cli/preview.hpp`, `cli/preview.cpp`, namespace `arcani::cli`, in `arcani_cli_core`).**

```cpp
// docs/signal-spec.md, Preview. Every function is pure apart from preview_layers
// (C API reads); none makes a transcendental call (sqrt only).

// Core, seconds-to-samples rule: x = t_s * fs (one rounding), then the nearest
// integer, ties to even; 0 outside the domain (negative, NaN, inf, x >= 2^64).
std::uint64_t seconds_to_samples(double t_s, std::uint32_t fs) noexcept;

// Preview, Gain: -440 <= P <= 0, m = P / 10.0 an integer, m * 10.0 == P.
bool preview_peak_valid(double peak_dbfs) noexcept;

// 10^(P / 20) by the Preview, Gain steps. Throws tables::TableError when
// !preview_peak_valid(peak_dbfs); the message names row preview-peak-level
// and the value.
double preview_amplitude(double peak_dbfs);

// g = preview_amplitude(P) / double(max(layers_max, 1)). Throws as above.
double preview_gain(double peak_dbfs, std::uint32_t layers_max);

struct PreviewRows {
  double length_s = 0.0;     // row preview-length
  double seam_tail_s = 0.0;  // row preview-seam-tail
  double peak_dbfs = 0.0;    // row preview-peak-level
};
// The three values from the given rows (Row::value()); throws
// tables::TableError when peak is not valid. main passes row(id) for each.
PreviewRows preview_rows(const arcani::test::tables::Row& length, const arcani::test::tables::Row& seam_tail,
                         const arcani::test::tables::Row& peak);

struct PreviewLayers {
  std::uint32_t left = 0;
  std::uint32_t right = 0;
  std::uint32_t max() const noexcept;  // the larger
};
// Per ear the distinct layers among that ear's rows of arc_plan_component.
// Returns false, with `rule` set to the failing call's rule, when an engine
// call fails (the caller exits 3 before any output).
bool preview_layers(const arc_plan* plan, PreviewLayers& out, std::string& rule);

// Preview, Frames: loop.looping == 0: min(length_frames, end_frame);
// else min(loop.opening_end_frame + loop.body_frames + tail_frames, end_frame).
std::uint64_t preview_frames(const arc_loop_frames& loop, std::uint64_t end_frame,
                             std::uint64_t length_frames, std::uint64_t tail_frames) noexcept;

// Each sample s becomes s * g (one binary64 multiplication), in place.
void apply_gain(std::span<double> interleaved, double g) noexcept;
```

**Previews tool (`tools/render_previews.py`).** Standard library only.

```
uv run --locked python tools/render_previews.py [--out-dir DIR] [--rate 44100|48000]
                                               [--renderer PATH] [--fixture PATH ...] [--list]
```

- Defaults: `--out-dir build/previews` (relative to the repository root, not the cwd), `--rate 48000`, `--renderer build/<preset>/cli/arcani-render[.exe]` with the preset picked as in `tests/python/test_spacing_engine.py` (`windows-clang-cl`, `macos-appleclang`, `linux-clang`), the fixtures `recipes/fixtures/valid/<name>` for each name of the preview set in vector order.

- Refusal: the output directory is resolved (`Path.resolve()`, so `..` and links are followed); if it is the repository root or inside it and not inside `<repo>/build`, print one line `render_previews: refused: <dir> is inside the repository but not under build/` on stderr and exit 2 before creating anything. A directory outside the repository is allowed.
- For each recipe, in order: run `<renderer> RECIPE --out <dir>/<stem>.preview.wav --rate <rate> --preview` (the report lands at `<dir>/<stem>.preview.report.json` by the CLI's default report path); on a non-zero exit, print the renderer's stderr and exit 1 at once. After each render print `<stem>: <frames> frames, <seconds> s, gain <gain>` from its report; at the end `render_previews: <count> previews, <total seconds> s of audio in <dir>`.
