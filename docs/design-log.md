- **superseded:** replaced by a later entry, which it names

### 2026-11-13 (qq): Gamma PAC layer, 50 ms bursts, the spectrum tool, and burst-center timing (S2.4)

- **Burst gate:** `docs/signal-spec.md`, Bursts. With `coupling` `bursts` the gamma AM depth is `d * g`: `g` is the smoothed on-off segment rule of the theta reference applied to `u = p_E - C mod 2^64`, with half width `Hb` (25 ms of theta envelope phase, `w = (50.0 / 1000.0 / 2) * rate_hz`, then `* 2^64`, nearest, ties to even) and edge `Wb` (10 ms, `w = (10.0 / 1000.0) * rate_hz`). So the 50 ms is the full width, each edge a 10 ms raised cosine inside it, a 30 ms plateau, centered on the coupling phase (entry (o)). `Wb < Hb - Wb` and `Hb < 2^63` over the whole theta-rate range. Depth law as the swell: `a = 1.0 - (d * g) * (1.0 - e)`. Off the burst `a = 1.0` exactly (the unmodulated carrier); on the plateau `a` is the uncoupled law bit for bit. The carrier always plays. The engine gate is `core::burst(theta_envelope_phase, C, Hb, Wb)`; a compile-time test pins its type (AD-5).
- **Range rows:** `burst-width` (50 ms) and `burst-edge` (10 ms): `pending` cleared, values unchanged; each source now also cites the signal spec Bursts section and this entry.
- **Gamma envelope runs free:** one clock, as uncoupled and swell; it does not restart at a burst. The research run `burst-edge-taper-2026-10-28` restarted the gamma AM at each burst center. At 60 Hz (a whole multiple of 6 Hz) both agree to within the phase increment rounding; at 40 and 45 Hz free running gives -59.0 and -58.7 dB against the run's -57.2 and -58.1 dB (worst gamma line at or above 394 Hz, 5 s, 48 kHz, build agent recompute).
- **Compile rules:** the three coupling rules of entry (nn) apply to `bursts`: AD-5 on `/gamma_pac/coupling` with value `bursts` and the same rule and limit; a missing phase with limit `present with coupling bursts`. Every `smooth_swell` message and every existing recipe hash is unchanged. `gamma-coupling-not-in-enum.json` now expects limit `uncoupled, smooth_swell, bursts`.
- **Error margin per tap** (by engine sine count, entry (ee)): gamma tap 3 sines at most (the edge cosine, the gamma envelope, the carrier), within `2 * b + 2^-46` (about -114 dBFS); bus with theta 5 sines, `3.5 * b + 2^-45` (about -109 dBFS); output with the binaural layer 6 sines, `4.5 * b + 2^-44` (about -107 dBFS). All under row `reference-match`.
- **Spectrum tool:** `tests/assertions/spectrum.py`, reads one tap and fs. 4-term Blackman-Harris window, zero-padded to at least 8 times the length, local maxima at least 90 dB under the strongest line, each refined by a parabola through the dB magnitudes of three bins; levels relative to the strongest line of the whole spectrum. New threshold rows `spectrum-frequency` (0.05 Hz) and `spectrum-level` (0.5 dB), decided by Audio on 2026-11-13, md row "Spectrum lines"; Story 3.1 reuses the tool and both rows unchanged. Calibrated first on Python-synthesized multi-tone signals of known lines.
- **Research figure match (Audio, 2026-11-13):** at 60 Hz the worst gamma line at or above 394 Hz equals the run's -51.3 dB within row `spectrum-level`; at 40 and 45 Hz it is no louder than -51.3 dB. 394 Hz is the 450 Hz theta reference's lowest line (444 Hz at 6 Hz) less the 50 Hz spacing placeholder. The worst 60 Hz line lies at 394 Hz exactly, so the band starts row `spectrum-frequency` below 394 Hz: a line read within the tool's frequency tolerance of the boundary counts as at it.
- **Burst-center timing:** `tests/assertions/coupling_timing.py`, `coupling="bursts"`. The smooth swell's theta-rate cosine fit gives each burst's first estimate; the center is then refined by a least-squares fit of `1 - |analytic|` of the gamma tap to `g(t - tau) * (c0 + c1 cos(w t) + c2 sin(w t))`, `w = 2 pi` times the recipe's gamma rate, `g` the ideal gate of rows `burst-width` and `burst-edge`. Reason: with a free-running gamma envelope a burst opens on a gamma phase that moves from cycle to cycle (240 degrees per theta cycle at 40 Hz), so the depth curve is not symmetric about the center and the cosine fit alone reads up to 1.65 ms off on Python-synthesized bursts: inside row `coupling-timing` but eight times row `coupling-timing-calibration`. This refines the story decision "the swell fit is reused"; Audio accepted it on 2026-11-13. A tap with no burst fails with note `no burst in gamma tap`.

### 2026-11-12 (pp): Binaural layer, beat rate cycle, and the isolation band and frequency phase modes (S2.5)

- **Binaural layer:** `docs/signal-spec.md`, Binaural layer. Two pure sines, one per ear: the left at `carrier_left_hz`, the right at `f_C = carrier_left_hz + beat_rate_hz` plus, with a beat rate cycle, the closed-form offset `U[n]`. The pair joins after every other stage and nothing reads it. Without a cycle the right ear is the Story 1.2 stub bit for bit, so every existing fixture, vector, and pinned hash keeps its bytes. A muted right ear also has no cycle, so it outputs `+0.0`.
- **Waveform:** new optional `binaural.waveform`, `sine` or `triangle` (absent means sine). Compile rejects `triangle`: parameter `/binaural/waveform`, rule `non-sine binaural not modeled`, limit `sine`, after every gamma and coupling rule (entry (o)).
- **Rate curve:** `docs/signal-spec.md`, Rate curve. One law, reused unchanged by Story 2.6: `rate(t) = r + s * sin(2 pi t / P + phi0)`, 0 degrees at the center rising. New optional `binaural.beat_rate_cycle` {`swing_hz`, `period_s`, `start_phase_deg`}; `beat_rate_hz` stays required and is the center. Engine: right phase `phase_C[n] + U[n] mod 2^64`, `U[n]` = cycles-to-units of `K * (c0 - cycle_cosine(p_P[n]))` with `c0 = cycle_cosine(P0)` (the precise cycle cosine below), `K = (s * P) * 0x1.45f306dc9c883p-3` (the binary64 nearest 1 / (2 pi)), `p_P[n] = P0 + n * inc(1 / P)`, `P0` by the coupling phase rule. The cycles-to-units rule reduces `v - trunc(v)` exactly and rounds `f * 2^64` to the nearest integer, ties to even, mod 2^64; `|v| >= 2^52`, NaN, and infinities give 0. Every sample is a pure function of `n`, so seek is one step. No piecewise-linear curves in v0.1.
- **Range checks, worst case:** after the existing binaural checks, on `/binaural/beat_rate_cycle/swing_hz`: `swing_hz >= 0` (rule `range.min`, limit `>= 0 Hz`), `r - s` and `r + s` against `beat-rate`, `(f_L + r) + s` against `binaural-carrier-right`; then `period_s` and `start_phase_deg` against the new rows.
- **New range rows:** `rate-cycle-period`, 60 to 60 s inclusive: the only period with a source (Lee et al. 2024, entry (n)), locked until a study supports others (Audio, 2026-11-11). `rate-cycle-start-phase`, 0 to 360 degrees inclusive, 360 the same point as 0: the whole cycle, an engineering default. Both cite this entry and the new md row "Binaural beat rate cycle". `coupling-independence`: `pending` cleared, value unchanged (0 samples), now read by the AD-5 sweep.
- **Sleep cycle:** center 1.5 Hz, swing 1.5 Hz, 60 s period, start phase 270 degrees: the beat rate cycles over 0 to 3 Hz every 60 s (entry (n)) and starts at 0 Hz, rising, so a session opens with no beat and it eases in. ### 2026-11-05 (ii): Command-line renderer, 24-bit rounding, report, and render speed (S1.4)

- **Usage and exit codes:** `arcani-render RECIPE --out WAV [--report JSON] [--rate 44100|48000] [--mode measure|final]`, or `--version` alone. Defaults: 48 kHz, `measure` (starters carry no stored gain until Epic 4), report beside the WAV with the last extension replaced by `.report.json`. Exit 0 rendered and every assertion pass or not applicable; 1 rejected by the engine (one stderr line `rejected: parameter=<p> value=<v> rule=<r> limit=<l>`); 2 usage; 3 file or internal error; 4 rendered with a failed assertion. A numeric `--rate` goes to `arc_compile`, so the engine's sample-rate rule refuses anything but 44.1 and 48 kHz.
- **No partial output:** compile runs before any file is opened; the WAV is written to `<WAV>.partial`, the report is written, then the partial file is renamed over the WAV path. A rejection or a failed render leaves nothing at the WAV path and never touches an existing file there.
- **24-bit rounding:** `docs/signal-spec.md`, 24-bit rounding (NaN gives 0; `x = s * 2^23`; `x >= 8388607` gives 8388607; `x <= -8388608` gives -8388608; else nearest, ties to even; 3 bytes little-endian; no dither). Each step is exact, so the result does not depend on the host rounding or flush mode (tested in all four rounding modes and with flush on). 35 vectors in `tests/data/signal_spec_vectors.json`; four naive conversions each fail a vector of their rule.
- **WAV layout:** `WAVE_FORMAT_PCM`, 16-byte `fmt `, 2 channels, 24 bits, block align 6, `data`; no other chunk. RIFF up to 715827876 frames (`36 + 6 * frames <= 0xFFFFFFFF`, about 4.1 h at 48 kHz); above that RF64 per EBU Tech 3306 (`ds64` with riffSize, dataSize, sampleCount = frames, table length 0). The header is a pure function of frame count and rate. 4096 frames per `arc_render` call (row `block-sizes` member; output does not depend on it).
- **Report:** UTF-8 JSON, members `engine_version`, `seed`, `sample_rate`, `mode`, `frames`, `determinism_key` (`recipe_hash`, `asset_hashes` empty in v0.1, `engine_version`, `sample_rate`), `assertions` (`name`, `row`, `threshold`, `unit`, `measured` or null, `result`, `note`). Shortest round-trip numbers (`std::to_chars`). New public getters `arc_plan_recipe_hash` and `arc_plan_seed`.
- **Render speed:** measured = (frames / rate) / summed `steady_clock` time of the `arc_render` calls (0 ticks counted as 1); pass at `>=` row `render-speed` (20 times real time). Measured only when `ARCANI_REFERENCE_MACHINE` is exactly `1` (Audio sets it once on the dev PC; no workflow or preset sets it, a test checks that); otherwise, and for 0 frames, `not_applicable`. Result on the dev PC: about 2100 times real time for the 1 s stub chain fixture at 48 kHz. Row `render-speed`: `pending` cleared, value unchanged.
- **Boundaries:** `cli/` links only `arcani::engine` (public header) and the assertion library (`tests/assertions`, AD-17), which therefore now builds in every job. `tools/check_cli_includes.py` fails any `cli/` compile command with an include path into `engine/src`; `tools/check_compile_flags.py` now applies every AD-9 rule to `cli/` sources too (closes the deferred review pass 1 finding 21 item).
- **Row usage file:** the CLI child processes the C++ tests start inherit `ARCANI_ROW_USAGE_FILE`. The assertion library now truncates that file only in the first process of a run (it then sets `ARCANI_ROW_USAGE_INHERITED` for its children) and every process adds its ids to what the file already holds, so a child no longer wipes the test binary's record.

### 2026-11-04 (gg): C API, internal block size, stub chain, and isolation measurement (S1.2)

- **API shape:** one `arc_` C API in `engine/include/arcani/arcani.h`, plain C (a C11 syntax check runs in ctest). Plan and renderer are separate handles: `arc_compile` returns an immutable plan with one reference (`arc_plan_retain`, `arc_plan_release`); `arc_renderer_create` does every allocation and holds its own plan reference; `arc_render` writes interleaved stereo binary64 plus five optional taps (theta reference, gamma PAC, stereo bus, binaural join, master) and never allocates or throws; `arc_seek` is pure; `arc_renderer_destroy`. Options: mode (`measure`, `final`), sample rate (44100 or 48000, anything else rejected with rule `sample_rate`), and `mute_binaural_ear` (an offline measurement control stored in the plan, not a recipe field). Errors are a C struct with four fixed 256-byte fields, truncated with a trailing `...`; status codes ok, invalid argument, rejected, out of memory, internal. `final` mode requires `loudness_gain_db` and `loudness_hash` (rule `final.required`); the hash match is Story 4.2. The warning list exists and is empty in v0.1.
- **Input cap:** recipe text over 1 MiB (1048576 bytes) is rejected with rule `input.max_bytes`; exactly 1 MiB compiles. Engineering bound, far above any recipe; closes Story 1.1 triage finding 18.
- **Internal block size:** 64 frames, a fixed engine constant (`docs/signal-spec.md`, Internal block size). Engineering default: it never shapes the signal, because every sample is a pure function of the plan and its index. Story 1.6 proves block-size invariance.
- **Stub levels:** theta reference and gamma PAC stubs output exact `+0.0`; the binaural join stub is one unmodulated sine per ear at unit amplitude from phase 0 (left ear `carrier_left_hz`, right ear `carrier_left_hz + beat_rate_hz`); per-channel master gain 1.0 and an identity stereo-linked limiter. These are stub values for the pipe, not starter levels; Epic 2 and Epic 4 replace them.
- **Floating-point mode guard:** every floating-point call (compile, renderer creation, render, seek) sets one fixed IEEE mode on entry with intrinsics (round to nearest, no flush, exceptions masked: MXCSR = 0x1F80 on x86-64; FPCR with RMode, FZ, DN, AH, FIZ, FZ16, and the trap enables cleared on AArch64) and restores the host's mode on every return. Any other target fails to compile. - **Isolation measurement:** muted-ear method in the C++ test harness. Over the whole render, a periodic Hann window and a single-bin DFT at the active ear's carrier; level = 20 log10(|X_muted| / |X_active|) in dB; pass when the level is at or below minus the row value; silent (fail) when the active carrier measures zero. - **Hash probe:** components `stub-chain-44100` and `stub-chain-48000` (400 Hz unmodulated sine left, 406 Hz unmodulated sine right, 1 s, through the C API), compared per run against windows-x64; expected-file entries stay with Story 1.6.

### 2026-11-04 (ff): Minimal recipe schema, strict JSON parser, and recipe hash (S1.1)

- **Fields:** `schema_version` ("MAJOR.MINOR", the schema pattern accepts major 1 only), `engine_version` ("X.Y.Z"), `seed`, `author`, `date` ("YYYY-MM-DD"), `rationale`, `length_s`, `loudness_target_lufs`, optional `loudness_gain_db` and `loudness_hash` (64 lowercase hex digits). - **Seed bound:** integer 0 to 2^53 - 1 (9007199254740991), the largest integer a JSON number holds exactly, so every tool reads the same seed. `7.0` is an integer (JSON Schema and the engine agree) and hashes as `7`.

- **Number conversion:** the engine converts decimal text to binary64 and back with its own exact big-integer arithmetic (correctly rounded reading, half to even; Burger and Dybvig shortest digits for writing, ECMAScript layout), not `std::from_chars` or `std::to_chars`, whose floating-point overloads are missing from the pinned Apple libc++. ### 2026-11-03 (bb): Signal spec core and sine rules, engine sine, probe sine components (S0.2)

- **Rounding rules (docs/signal-spec.md, Core):** phase increment `inc` = `q` rounded to the nearest integer, ties to even, where `q = (f_hz * 2^64) / fs` in binary64 (exact multiply, one rounded division); out of domain (`f_hz < 0`, `f_hz >= fs/2`, NaN, infinities) gives 0. Seconds to samples: `n` = `t_s * fs` in binary64, rounded to the nearest integer, ties to even; negative, NaN, infinite, or a product at or above 2^64 gives 0 (the spec's own choice for the uint64 limit, mirroring the increment rule). Ties to even matches IEEE 754 default rounding and Python's `round`, so the engine and the reference need no special case.
- **Engine sine:** exact integer fold of the 64-bit phase to `[-2^62, 2^62]`, one int64-to-binary64 conversion, a multiply by the binary64 nearest to `2*pi/2^64`, then an odd Taylor polynomial to x^11 in a fixed Horner order, coefficients the binary64 nearest to `+-1/k!` as hex floats. Computed truncation bound `(pi/2)^13/13!`, about 5.7e-8 (-145 dBFS). Measured: max error 5.63e-8 (-145.0 dBFS) over the spec test resolution (2^24 phases plus each quadrant edge and its neighbours), clang-cl 23.1.4, against the -120 dBFS bound (entry 2026-10-28 (o)).
- **Quantization and drift:** worst-case increment error `|inc - f*2^64/fs|` is at most 32.5 phase units for carriers and rates up to 1030 Hz, so frequency error is at most about 8.5e-14 Hz at 48 kHz, against the 0.01 Hz threshold; 8-hour drift is at most about 2.4e-8 s (worst at the 0.001 Hz grid floor of the 0 Hz ranges; about 3e-11 s or less at the endpoints of the ranges with a floor of 1 Hz or more) for every range row in `tests/tables/ranges.json` measured on the C++ 0.001 Hz grid and exactly at every range endpoint in Python, against 1 ms. Ranges with a 0 Hz floor use the 0.001 Hz grid step as their lowest nonzero frequency for the analytic drift bound (relative drift has no bound as f goes to 0; at exactly 0 Hz, `inc = 0` and there is no drift).
- **Probe components:** `sine-44100` and `sine-48000`, the engine sine, unmodulated, 1 s each of a 1 Hz, a 450 Hz, and a 1000 Hz carrier, phase 0 at each segment start, float32. They are compared exactly against `windows-x64` by the existing full tier; they have no `ci/hash_expected.json` entry (that file is unchanged), and the Python test checks the host-built probe against the ideal sine within -120 dBFS. ### 2026-11-02 (z): Engine compile flags, warning flags, and undefined-symbol denylist (AD-9)

- **Decision:** the top-level CMake adds `-ffp-contract=off` (clang-cl `/clang:-ffp-contract=off`) for the whole project with `add_compile_options`, so it lands after `CMAKE_CXX_FLAGS` and the build-type flags on every command line and restores contraction off after any earlier fp option. Other fast-math effects of an earlier flag are not undone; the flag check catches them. No fast-math flag is set anywhere. `arcani_engine` and `arcani-render` build with warnings as errors: clang `-Wall -Wextra -Werror`, clang-cl `/W4 /WX`. C++20 module scanning is off (`CMAKE_CXX_SCAN_FOR_MODULES OFF`): no modules are used, and the scan adds an `@` modmap response file to every compile command, which would hide flags from the check.
- **Checks:** `tools/check_compile_flags.py` reads the build's `compile_commands.json` and fails, naming toolchain, source file, and flag, when an `engine/` source's effective contraction is not off, a fast-math flag appears, a value-changing fp option appears, a command uses an `@` response file, or there are no engine sources. Flags count bare, behind `/clang:` or `-clang:`, or after `-Xclang`. Effective contraction: clang-cl moves every `/clang:` argument after all other arguments, so those are evaluated last; the value is then set by the last of `-ffp-contract=X` or an fp model that implies contraction on (`-ffp-model=precise|fast|aggressive`, `/fp:precise|fast`). The strict and except models never turn contraction on (verified with `clang-cl -###`). ### 2026-10-28 (o): Reference sine, depth-gated bursts, burst edges, non-sine binaural

- **Decision:** the Python reference renders the ideal sine. The signal spec states the ideal waveform plus the engine approximation's error bound: at most -120 dBFS against the ideal. Reason: a reference that implements the engine's own polynomial cannot catch a bad sine. Margin arithmetic: three layers' errors adding coherently gain about 9.5 dB, leaving about 10 dB under the -100 dBFS reference match. This refines AD-19 (one formula source) while it is REVIEW PENDING; the independent reviewer should check it.
- **Decision:** gamma bursts and swells are depth-gated. The gamma PAC carrier plays continuously; bursts and swells gate only its AM depth, matching the CAP-4 wording. Audio: it "would sound better and be less abrupt." Carrier-gated rectangular bursts at 40 Hz fail spacing against the 450 Hz theta reference (worst line -21 dB).
- **Decision:** the 50 ms burst width (Alekseichuk et al. 2016) is the full width. Each edge is a 10 ms raised cosine inside it, leaving a 30 ms plateau. Computed spectra: worst line at or above 394 Hz is -51.3 dB at 60 Hz gamma, the worst case checked across 40, 45, and 60 Hz. 10 ms is the top of the 5 to 10 ms rise-time convention in auditory physiology. No acoustic study sets burst edges.
- **Decision:** a non-sine binaural waveform is rejected in v0.1 with a named rule (parameter `binaural.waveform`, rule "non-sine binaural not modeled", limit sine). The CAP-5 compile warning applies once the signal spec models non-sine binaural. Resolves a contradiction between SPEC CAP-5 and the SPEC Assumptions.
- **Confidence:** weak for the burst edges (engineering default from computed spectra and a physiology convention); the -120 dBFS bound is an engineering default.
- **Source:** research run `burst-edge-taper-2026-10-28` (literature notes and `burst_spectra.py`); Alekseichuk et al. 2016 methods.
- **Decided by:** Audio, during the v0.1 story breakdown session (sine reference and margin, depth-gated, burst width and edges). The non-sine binaural rule was delegated by Audio to the build agents.
- **Status:** engineering defaults and proposed, as listed in Current state. Phase 0 may replace any value; S2.4 must reproduce the burst figures through the signal spec, reference, and validator.

### 2026-10-27 (b): Coupling phase definition and default

- **Decision:** the coupling phase is a recipe parameter from 0 to 360 degrees, defined on the in-file theta reference envelope, with 0 degrees at the envelope maximum.
  - Default: 0 degrees (peak).
  - Recipes can choose uncoupled gamma as a control.
  - The coupling timing test checks this phase in the sound, which is the only phase that is measurable and reproducible.
- **Evidence:**
  - **tACS studies:** peak-coupled gamma helped in some of them, and trough-coupled gamma was ineffective or impaired performance. But the direct peak vs trough contrast was not significant, and one peak-coupled study reduced accuracy.
  - **Brain recordings:** they show no single preferred phase.
  - **Sound:** no study has tested theta-gamma coupled sound.
  - **Delay:** cortical tracking of theta AM lags the sound by roughly 80 to 160 ms, and the lag varies by listener. So the sound phase is a design convention, not a brain target.
- **Confidence:** low to medium.
- **Source:** research run `academic-lit-gamma-rate-and-coupling-phase-2026-10-27`, refs [13] to [23].
- **Status:** proposed. Needs an independent review before it locks (CLAUDE.md rule 4). The Phase 1 EEG pilot (peak vs trough vs uncoupled sound, with each listener's delay measured) would make it evidence-based.
- **No claims:** no source supports an outcome claim for any coupling phase delivered by sound.

### 2026-10-27 (a): Gamma AM rate default and range

- **Decision:** default 40 Hz, locked range 30 to 60 Hz. Any rate in the range is valid.
  - Treat 60 to 80 Hz as a possible extended band later, only if auditory behavioral evidence appears.
  - With the 4 times carrier rule, a 60 Hz rate needs a carrier of at least 240 Hz.
- **Evidence:**
  - The human auditory steady-state response peaks in the 30 to 40 Hz region.
  - Individual peak rates measured 36 to 53 Hz, with a mean of about 42 to 46 Hz.
  - About 58 of 62 auditory gamma studies used 40 Hz. Data outside 40 Hz is sparse: 60 Hz mainly from one lab, and one study at 80 Hz.
  - Behavioral effects in healthy adults are inconsistent even at 40 Hz.
- **Confidence:** high for the response peak, low for behavioral relevance.
- **Listening check:** on carriers of a few hundred Hz, roughness peaks broadly at 20 to 50 Hz, so 40 Hz may sound rough. Add this to the starter recipe listening checklist.
- **Future feature:** a per-listener gamma rate within the range is supported (refs [5][6]). It is not a v0.1 default.
- **Source:** research run `academic-lit-gamma-rate-and-coupling-phase-2026-10-27`, refs [1] to [12].
- **Status:** proposed. Needs an independent review before it locks.

### 2026-10-21: Gaps check outcomes (baseline)

These are recorded from `docs/project-plan.md`, Gaps check. The design log started on 2026-10-27, so this is the earliest baseline entry.

- **Theta reference:** added as an in-file AM carrier, the only thing PAC couples to (Orozco Perez et al., 2020).
- **Binaural layer:** kept as an optional layer, with its default still open (Orozco Perez et al., 2020; López-Caballero and Escera, 2017).
- **Binaural locked ranges:** carriers below 1000 Hz and a difference under about 35 Hz (Licklider et al., 1950, as cited by López-Caballero and Escera). So a gamma-rate beat can never be binaural.
- **Carrier spacing rule and validator:** added, so that a theta reference sharing a carrier with the binaural layer cannot leak the other ear's tone.
- **Alamalhoda et al. (2025):** dropped as support for audio, because it used light flicker.
