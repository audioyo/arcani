# Research library

Every source from the Monroe Technical Reference (April 2026), plus sources added since, checked against what each study actually tested. Of the 36 entries in the first review, 16 hold up (12 as cited, 4 with small corrections), 6 were overstated, 4 could not be located, 1 is preliminary, and 9 are context or standards rather than evidence. Checked October 21, 2026.

## What the research says, in short

1. **PAC is real, but the evidence is about the brain's own rhythms.** Trough coupling and encoding come from electrodes inside the hippocampus, mostly in rodents or epilepsy patients. No source tests whether sound designed with coupling makes a listener's brain couple.
2. **Sound that pulses in the file beats binaural beats for entrainment.** Monaural beats drive the cortex more strongly; binaural results are mixed, with one null EEG study. Binaural beats do produce a unique cross-frequency effect.
3. **The case for 60 Hz over 40 Hz is weaker than v5 read.** It rested on rodent band roles that the main review calls hypotheses, and on a light-flicker study. The only auditory 60 Hz study found one narrow effect.
4. **The trough default is unproven.** Lega et al. found trough coupling with 2.5 to 5 Hz theta, below the 6 Hz default. The only human study that delivered coupled stimulation (electrical) found peak-aligned gamma helped working memory and trough-aligned gamma cancelled the benefit.
5. **Movement itself may matter.** Sudre et al. (2024) attribute their effects to the sounds moving in space, not to beating, which supports the Orbit layer.
6. **Lower carriers drive larger gamma responses.** The response grows about 30% per octave down, so 250 to 300 Hz beats 450 Hz for a gamma layer (Ross et al., 2003).
7. **Behavioral effects are modest and need time.** The binaural meta-analysis found a medium effect overall, larger with longer exposure.

Status key: **Verified** = the source says what we use it for. **Corrected** = real source, details fixed here. **Overstated** = real source, but it doesn't support the v5 claim. **Not located** = couldn't be found; don't cite until it is. **Context** = background, standard, or tradition, not evidence for a design choice.

## Coupled stimulation and carrier effects

Added October 21 to answer two questions: which carriers work best, and which coupling phase to use. The one human study that delivered theta-gamma coupled stimulation found the opposite phase from the v5 default.
